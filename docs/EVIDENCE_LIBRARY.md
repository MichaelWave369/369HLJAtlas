# Evidence Library: editorial provenance and interpretation

**Reviewed for starter editorial framing:** 2026-11-09. This is not a completed scientific or systematic review.

## Data contract

`site/data/research.json` contains source records and 10 standalone dossiers, keyed to existing topic IDs. Every record is human-readable, versioned, and browser-local. An external source page is opened only when clicked; we do not embed third-party scripts or collect browsing data.

The `classification` describes a **narrow focus**, not a general verdict on a culture, practice or whole topic. `supported` summarizes what linked references establish in scope. `boundary` explicitly identifies an overreach, missing evidence or methodological limit. `alternative` and `nextTest` preserve falsifiability. A source can be background or counterevidence; do not assume it supports every sentence. `sourceIds` are pointers for manual inspection, not proof that the Atlas independently replicated a paper.

## Editorial review protocol

1. Check every outgoing URL, title, study type, and date; use a null year rather than inventing metadata.
2. Match each supported finding and limitation to the correct cited record, and preserve contrary results.
3. Distinguish an observed physical process from a separate claim that it heals or alters biology.
4. For culturally specific traditions, identify authors, custodians and chronology; seek permission before presenting cultural protocols as a universal technique.
5. Label sources representing a different experimental protocol rather than calling them direct replications. Telepathy's Ganzfeld meta-analysis and Transparent Psi experiment are specifically different paradigms.
6. Add adverse events and limitations before recommending any wellness practice, and never recommend postponing medical treatment.
7. Require independent review before promoting a dossier to a stronger evidence classification or expanding health claims.
