# Research Charter

The purpose of this Atlas is a respectful bridge across scientific, cultural and spiritual inquiry. Different perspectives deserve a fair hearing; different factual claims do not automatically deserve the same evidentiary confidence.

1. **Scientific:** define narrow testable claims, cite primary literature, acknowledge uncertainty, include negative evidence and reproducibility considerations.
2. **Experimental:** prestate predictions, controls and alternatives, preserve failed trials, distinguish visual simulations from measurements.
3. **Cultural:** attribute specific traditions and communities, avoid conflating Indigenous cultures, distinguish symbolic meaning from demonstrated physical mechanism.

All imported founding topics begin **not-reviewed**; this is not a verdict about their validity. Future claim-level evidence states must include dated sources and clear limits.

The Atlas is educational, not a health service. Do not infer that terms such as DNA activation, frequency healing, orgonite, psychic powers or water memory constitute clinically validated mechanisms. No proposed experiment should encourage dangerous stunts, harmful exposures or replacement of professional care. Any future biometrics must be optional, consent-based and non-diagnostic.

Examine institutions and historical claims narrowly using attributable records, avoiding guilt by association or unwarranted global coordination narratives. Distinguish allegations, confirmed wrongdoing and speculation.

HLJ is a philosophy of **Hope · Love · Joy**, not a statement of endorsement by HOPEvolve or any individual. Respect attribution and require agreement before describing someone as a collaborator.

The current page is a static research registry. It does not measure fields, modify DNA, heal illnesses or transmit signals. Existing laboratory links are external links, not live integration assertions.
