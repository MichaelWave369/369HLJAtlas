# 369 HLJ Atlas

**Hope · Love · Joy | A bridge between ancient wisdom and modern discovery.**

We do not require agreement to explore together. This repository will host a local-first, source-conscious interactive atlas of geometry, resonance, consciousness, culture, community, and human potential.

> Knowledge is an invitation to inquire, not a declaration that every extraordinary claim is established.

Initial application implementation is proposed through a feature pull request. 

## Principles

- Distinguish established science, research hypotheses, health claims, and cultural or spiritual practices.
- Support careful experiments, diverse perspectives, citations, counterevidence, and clear limitations.
- Create links to existing Phi369 Labs projects instead of copying their functionality.
- Preserve reader privacy and keep the public experience accessible without accounts.
